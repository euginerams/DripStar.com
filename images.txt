DRIPSTARS.COM — product photo filenames
==================================================

HOW THIS WORKS
---------------
1. In your website folder, create a folder named exactly: images
2. For each photo, rename it to the EXACT filename shown below,
   then move it into the images folder.
3. .jpg is preferred, but .jpeg, .png and .webp also work
   automatically. Just match the name before the dot exactly.
4. Filenames are case-sensitive, lowercase, no spaces.
5. A product with more than one color needs one photo per color.
6. No photo yet for something? Skip it — the illustration shows
   until a matching file appears.
